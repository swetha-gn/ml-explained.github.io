## Hey, I'm Swetha Gendlur Nagarajan

ML engineer and researcher. I write here every day about machine learning, research papers, and ideas.
I build AI systems that work in the real world — where the physics matters, the hardware has limits, and engineers need the model to fit into their existing workflow.

I am an MS Applied Data Science student at the University of Florida with a 4.0 GPA, graduating in May 2026. Before that, I earned my BE in Computer Science and Engineering from PSG College of Technology.

My current work sits at the intersection of machine learning, photonics, semiconductor design, and deployment. I am especially interested in making expensive computation accessible through physics-informed models, surrogate digital twins, GPU acceleration, quantization, and practical MLOps.

## Current focus

- **Physics-informed ML:** digital twins, PINNs, and models that respect the constraints of physical systems.
- **Hardware-aware deployment:** CUDA, quantization, ONNX Runtime, and the details that decide whether a model is actually fast.
- **Workflow-centered AI:** systems that disappear into the tools people already use instead of asking users to change how they work.
- **Technical writing:** visual explainers for research papers, engineering systems, and complex ideas.

## Selected highlights

- First-author peer-reviewed paper presented at **SPIE Photonics West 2026**: “From ML to Physics-Informed Neural Networks: Scalable Digital Twin for Stress-Aware Design in Photonic Structures.”
- Built a stress-aware photonic digital twin surrogate with **8,344× speedup** and **R²=0.9999**, deployed through ONNX inside FLOOPS.
- Built healthcare RAG systems over **12M records** with **95% accuracy** and **40% faster retrieval**.
- Developed CUDA batch inference with INT8/INT6 quantization and **1,932× speedup**.
